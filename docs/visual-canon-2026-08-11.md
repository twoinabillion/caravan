# 서울까지 400km — visual canon

The four files in `assets/reference/` are production constraints, not loose mood references.

## Dalguji

- A weathered Korean cab-over one-ton truck with a separate, box-shaped camper body.
- Warm gray-beige paint, a darker dust-stained lower body, individually framed amber windows, rear double doors, and a faded hand-painted white `X`.
- Black/olive soft cargo and two red fuel cans remain strapped to the roof rack. The antenna and exterior rails remain visible.
- It must never become a panel van, American RV, bus, pickup camper, blue Porter, or integrated step-van.
- Upgrades may lengthen the rear living box and add practical storage, armor, or bunks. The cab, paint, white `X`, roof kit, windows, and rear-door identity remain fixed.

## Interior

- A narrow hand-built mobile home: warm amber lantern, wood floor and walls, rear bunk, map/work table, radio, metal storage, and Leo's guitar after he joins.
- Warm light is a practical interior accent. Exterior daylight remains cool and restrained.
- The interior must read as the inside of the same cab-over box camper, never a generic cargo van or luxury RV.

- Faces, ages, hair, signature garments, and carried props do not change between portrait, event, introduction, and trailer art.

## World

- Near-photographic cinematic concept art of post-collapse South Korea; human scale, believable Korean roads, signs, ports, markets, apartments, and industrial infrastructure.
- Cool gray-blue, desaturated exterior palette with wet asphalt, haze, fog, smoke, or overcast light. Amber light appears selectively in inhabited interiors and night shelters.
- Busan is a bleak working port; highways are elevated and damaged; mountain roads are wet and fog-bound; markets are dark covered structures; industrial roads feel heavy and rain-soaked; Seoul is muted blue along the Han and Namsan.
- Avoid anime, pixel art, glossy sci-fi, fantasy ruins, sunny pastoral warmth, neon cyberpunk, and clean modern vehicles.

## Framing rules

- When the vehicle appears, its silhouette and fixed identifiers must be readable even at small size.
- Character action frames keep the canonical face and outfit while changing only pose, expression, camera, weather, and action.
- No embedded captions, labels, UI, watermarks, or decorative text inside scene art.
